Privacy Policy – Artist Commission Tracker

Last updated: December 1, 2026

Artist Commission Tracker is a browser extension and web dashboard that helps freelance artists keep track of their commissions. This page explains what it does with your data.

What the extension reads

Only when you click Scan this page on vgen.co, x.com, twitter.com or discord.com, the extension reads the text you have selected on that page. If nothing is selected, it reads a limited amount of the visible page text instead. It looks for a price and a deadline. This analysis happens inside your browser, and the text it reads is not sent anywhere.

The selected text can include messages written by other people, for example a client in a Discord chat. The extension does not send those messages to the server. Only the fields listed below leave your browser, and only after you have reviewed them and pressed Sync.

What is stored on our server

  • Your account: your email address and your password, stored only as a salted hash (bcrypt), never as plain text.
  • Each commission you sync: a title (it starts as the first line of your selection, and you can edit it before syncing), a budget, a deadline, the platform name, a link to the page, the status you set, and a confidence score from the scanner.
  • For Discord pages, the link also carries a short code derived from the text you selected. It is used only to tell two different messages in the same chat apart, so the same message is not saved twice.
  • Alert records: which of your commissions have already triggered a deadline alert.

What stays on your device

The extension keeps your login token and your alert-frequency setting in the browser's local extension storage. Deadline alerts are shown as system notifications.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data or share it with third parties.
  • We do not show ads and we do not use analytics or tracking tools.
  • We do not read pages in the background. The extension reads a page only when you click Scan this page.
  • We use your data only to provide the commission-tracking features described here.

Where your data is kept

The server runs on Render and the database runs on Neon, both in Singapore. Data travels between your browser and the server over HTTPS.

Permissions the extension asks for

  • storage: to remember your login and settings.
  • alarms and notifications: to check for upcoming deadlines and show alerts.
  • Access to vgen.co, x.com, twitter.com and discord.com: so the Scan button can read the text you select on those sites.
  • Access to our server and our dashboard website: to save and load your commissions, and so a notification can bring you back to a dashboard tab you already have open instead of opening a new one.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data is kept until you ask for it to be deleted. To delete your account and everything stored with it, email CommishTracker@proton.me.

Children

This service is not directed at children.
